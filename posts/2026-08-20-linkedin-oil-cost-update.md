## Draft

Brent crude traded at $91.87 a barrel in early Thursday trading, up slightly
and the fourth straight session of gains. Reuters reports the move came as
shipping through the Strait of Hormuz slowed further and the UAE suspended
financial and economic ties with Iran — two more signs that this war isn't
close to resolved.

We track this separately using the EIA's daily Brent price. By that measure,
oil is running about 64% above the $58 a barrel the EIA had projected for
2026 before the war started. Carried forward day by day since late February,
that gap now adds up to more than $143 billion in extra U.S. petroleum
spending — growing by roughly $8,900 every second.
