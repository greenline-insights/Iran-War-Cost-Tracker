How the number is built

Every calendar day since February 27, 2026 carries the most recent EIA daily Brent spot price; weekends and holidays inherit the prior trading day. Each day adds one 365th of the implied annual excess spending. The live ticker counts forward from midnight Eastern after the newest observation at the latest per-second rate.

Daily damage
max(0, $435,467M × (Brent ÷ $58 − 1)) ÷ 365
Live rate
max(0, $435,467M × (latest ÷ $58 − 1)) ÷ 31,536,000 s
